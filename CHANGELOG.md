# Changelog

Written for players and pack authors: what the Personal Assembler does, and what a pack can set.

## Unreleased

- Assembler 2 and 3 take any Assembling recipe that names a fluid, in or out, on the fluid machinery the Chemical Plant and the Oil Refinery share: two input fluid boxes and three output fluid boxes, bound by order (a recipe's `n`th fluid ingredient goes in input box `n`, its `n`th fluid result in output box `n`) and sized as theirs are. Where they had one fluid box that took one ingredient and never made fluid, a recipe may now have up to two fluid ingredients and three fluid results, with or without items, in any category the tier holds. While the Held recipe names a fluid each Fluid Connection pulls the fluid ingredients into their boxes and pushes the fluid results out of theirs, and a craft waits while an output box can't hold what it makes. The first fluid result takes the room of the output boxes a recipe leaves unused unless it is Pinned, and an Assembler has three where the Chemical Plant has two, so a recipe with one fluid result holds at least 300 mB of it, where a Chemical Plant holds 200. Assembler 1 still takes no fluid recipe. (#40)
- Assembler 2 and 3 have six Fluid Connections where they had two: all three bottom-layer blocks along the edge they face and along the opposite one, the two edge centres they had and the four corners the Chemical Plant has, each a grey ring on the casing. A connection has no direction and a pipe network carries one fluid, so a recipe of five different fluids needs a connection for each. (#40)
- Fill Recipe on Assembler 2 and 3 is refused for a recipe with more than two fluid ingredients or more than three fluid results, no longer for one with two fluid ingredients or a fluid result, and Assembler 1 refuses any recipe that names a fluid. The two messages say so. (#40)
- The screen of Assembler 2 and 3 shows a gauge for each of its five fluid boxes, inputs then outputs, and the energy bar below, and is a row taller; Assembler 1's is as it was. (#40)
- Jade's provider for an Assembler's fluid bars is now `craftworks:assembler_fluids` where it was `craftworks:assembler_fluid`, so a player's Jade toggle for it resets once, and it draws each box the Held recipe binds a fluid to and any that holds some. (#40)
- An Assembler saved with the old one fluid box keeps its fluid: it loads into the first input box, left as it is if it is over the box's new size, and is saved under a new key, so a world saved by this version keeps no fluid when loaded by an older one. A swap between Assembler 2 and 3 keeps all five boxes, and one to Assembler 1 voids them. (#40)

## 0.5.0

- An Assembling recipe may leave out `ingredients` and `results` when they are empty, as a recipe that only uses and makes fluids does, such as an Oil Refinery's; before, a recipe without either key failed to load. A recipe that makes nothing, no item and no fluid, is still refused. (#27)
- Craftworks accepts Pipeworks 0.2.0 or later beside it, an optional dependency, and refuses to load with an older one. From 0.2.0 a Pipeworks pipe beside a Fluid Connection draws an arm toward it while the connection exists, and loses it when the Held recipe no longer names a fluid. (Pipeworks #11)
- The Chemical Plant's and Assembler 2 and 3's fluid boxes follow the FactoryWorks Pack's Factorio figures, so they change from 0.4.2. An output box starts at 100 mB and holds the larger of that and 3 crafts' worth of the result bound to it, where it held the larger of 1,000 mB and the Overload Limit's crafts' worth; the first fluid result also takes the 100 mB of each output box the recipe leaves unused, so a plant on a recipe with one fluid result holds at least 200 mB of it (the larger of 200 mB and 3 crafts' worth). An input box still holds at most 1,000 mB but is filled, by the machine's own pull and by a pipe or mod that pushes, only to 4 crafts' worth of the ingredient bound to it whatever the crafting speed, so a tier 2 Assembler on a recipe of 10 mB a craft holds 40 mB, not a bucket. The screens' gauges and Jade's bars show each box's volume as it now stands, and a craft still waits while an output box can't hold what it makes. These figures are constants, not config. Jade's provider for the Chemical Plant's fluid bars is now `craftworks:fluid_machine_fluids` where it was `craftworks:chemical_plant_fluids`, so a player's Jade toggle for it resets once. (#39)
- An Assembling recipe has an optional `pinned_fluid_results`, false when omitted (`pinned_fluid_results` in KubeJS): a Pinned recipe keeps each fluid result to its own output box, the larger of 100 mB and 3 crafts' worth, instead of letting the first result take the boxes the recipe leaves unused. Factorio's basic oil processing is one. (#39)
- The Oil Refinery (`craftworks:oil_refinery`), a placed machine for `oil-processing` recipes (`oil-processing` only by default, set in `craftworks-server.toml`), on the Chemical Plant's machinery. Craftworks ships no recipes for it: a pack supplies them, such as one that splits crude oil into heavy oil, light oil and petroleum gas. It stands on a 5x5 footprint three blocks tall, is placed and broken whole from one item, and keeps its Held recipe on the item. It has no item slots, two input fluid boxes and three output fluid boxes, bound by order and sized as the Chemical Plant's are. Fill Recipe is refused, with a message, for a recipe outside its categories, with any item ingredient or item result, with more than two fluid ingredients or three fluid results, or with more than 1,000 mB of an ingredient a craft. Its five Fluid Connections, with no direction of their own, stand two on the bottom-layer edge it faces and three on the opposite one, turn with it, and show as the same grey ring on the casing; they exist only while the Held recipe names a fluid, in or out, and each pulls the ingredients into their boxes and pushes the results out of theirs. Its figures are in the new `oil_refinery` section: speed 1, 210 FE a tick (Factorio's 420 kW), a 50,000 FE buffer, and the categories. It has a recipe of its own, `craftworks:oil_refinery`: 4 iron ingots, 2 iron blocks, 2 cauldrons and a Chemical Plant, which a pack may replace. Its screen shows the five boxes as gauges and the energy, and Jade, EMI and JEI treat it as they do the Chemical Plant. `oil-processing` recipes now show in the Oil Refinery's tab (`craftworks:oil_refinery`, with the Oil Refinery its only workstation) in EMI and JEI, where by default they showed in no tab. Stand-in art. (#27)

## 0.4.2

- EMI and JEI show one tab for each machine in place of the single Assembling tab: Assembler (`craftworks:assembler`, shared by the three tiers) and Chemical Plant (`craftworks:chemical_plant`). A recipe is in every tab whose machine holds its category under the server config, so by default `crafting`, `advanced-crafting` and `crafting-with-fluid` are in the Assembler's tab and `chemistry` in the Chemical Plant's, and a recipe both hold is in both. A category no machine holds (`oil-processing` by default) is in no tab; the log says how many recipes were left out each time the lists are built. Each machine is a workstation of its own tab only, so `U` on an Assembler opens the Assembler tab and `U` on the Chemical Plant opens its tab; the tabs are read from the server config when EMI and JEI build their lists, so an edit shows after the next recipe reload. Fill Recipe and `+` show on both tabs with a machine open, and the Personal Assembler's Fill Recipe works from either. EMI favourites and recipe-tree entries saved under the old `craftworks:assembling` tab are lost once. A resource pack's tab title under `emi.category.craftworks.assembling` or `jei.category.craftworks.assembling` moves to `.assembler` and `.chemical_plant`. (#38)
- The Chemical Plant (`craftworks:chemical_plant`), a placed machine built on the Assembler's Held recipe, energy and Fluid Connections, for `chemistry` recipes (`chemistry` only by default, set in `craftworks-server.toml`). Craftworks ships no recipes for it: a pack supplies them. It stands on the Assembler's 3x3, two blocks tall, is placed and broken whole from one item, and keeps its Held recipe on the item. It has two input slots and a product slot, two input fluid boxes of 1,000 mB and two output fluid boxes: a recipe's `n`th fluid ingredient goes in input box `n` and its `n`th fluid result in output box `n`, and an output box holds the larger of 1,000 mB and the Overload Limit's crafts' worth of its result. Fill Recipe is refused, with a message, for a recipe outside its categories, with more than two item ingredients, fluid ingredients or fluid results, with more than 1,000 mB of an ingredient a craft, with more than one item result, or with an ingredient that leaves a remainder. A craft waits while an output box can't hold what it makes. Its four Fluid Connections stand two to an edge on two opposite bottom edges of the machine, turn with it, and show as the same grey ring on the casing; they exist only while the Held recipe names a fluid, in or out. Each pulls the Held recipe's fluid ingredients from the tank or pipe it touches into their boxes and pushes its fluid results out of theirs, so a pipe never has to push into it or pull out of it. Its figures are in the new `chemical_plant` section: speed 1, 105 FE a tick (Factorio's 210 kW), a 50,000 FE buffer, and the categories. It has a recipe of its own, `craftworks:chemical_plant`: 4 iron ingots, 2 glass blocks, 2 cauldrons and an Assembler 1, which a pack may replace. Its screen shows the four boxes as gauges and the energy, and Jade, EMI and JEI treat it as they do an Assembler. Stand-in art. (#26)
- Jade, if installed, draws the Creative Fluid Source's "Water, infinite" or "No fluid" and the Creative Energy Source's "Infinite FE" in white on their bars, as it draws any tank's fluid, where they were dark.
- A resource pack's translations of the screen's energy and fluid text move to new keys, since the Chemical Plant's screen shows them too: `craftworks.assembler.energy`, `craftworks.assembler.fluid` and `craftworks.assembler.fluid_empty` become `craftworks.machine.energy`, `craftworks.machine.fluid` and `craftworks.machine.fluid_empty`.

## 0.4.1

- A player whose connection never opened Craftworks' channels, such as the mock player another mod's game tests make, is sent none of its payloads, where logging one in used to fail with `Payload craftworks:assembling_recipes may not be sent to the client!`. Game tests that load Craftworks beside a pack's own mods no longer fail for it.
- The Creative Fluid Source (`craftworks:creative_fluid_source`), a creative-only block for feeding an Assembler's Fluid Connections where no fluid mod is installed, such as the dev client: it holds one fluid, or none when placed. Use a filled bucket on it to set its fluid (the bucket stays full, in creative or survival) and an empty bucket to clear it; the fluid is kept over a reload and the item placed back keeps none. Its capability, on all six faces, gives its fluid without limit and accepts none, and it never pushes: an Assembler pulls from it as from any tank. It has no recipe, so EMI and JEI show none, and it drops itself when broken. Find it in the Craftworks creative tab after the Creative Energy Source, with stand-in art for now, and Jade, if installed, shows its fluid as, say, "Water, infinite", or "No fluid". (#32)
- EMI and JEI show an Assembling recipe's fluid ingredients, each in the input row after the item ingredients as a fluid slot with its amount on hover, and the recipe is wider for it. In EMI a fluid ingredient is a real input, so looking up the fluid's uses lists the recipe. A recipe with no fluid ingredient looks as before. (#34)
- EMI and JEI draw an Assembling recipe at least as wide as its widest line of text, so the category (`Category: crafting-with-fluid`) and the seconds sit inside the recipe and no longer run over the buttons beside it. (#33)
- Assembler 2 and 3 craft recipes with a fluid ingredient, taking the fluid through two Fluid Connections at the centres of two opposite bottom edges of the machine. They turn with it and show as a grey ring on the casing, and exist only while the Held recipe has a fluid ingredient. Each pulls the fluid the Held recipe needs, and only that, from whatever tank or pipe touches it (anything with NeoForge's fluid capability) into the Assembler's one 1,000 mB fluid box; an Assembler never pushes fluid, though a mod that pushes can fill the box through a connection. (#25)
- Fill Recipe on Assembler 2 and 3 is refused, with a message, for a recipe with two fluid ingredients or one needing more than 1,000 mB of its fluid a craft, and still for any recipe with a fluid result. Assembler 1 still takes no fluid recipe. (#25)
- The fluid box takes only the Held recipe's fluid and is kept over a reload and a swap between Assembler 2 and 3. It is emptied when the Held recipe changes, on a swap to Assembler 1 and when the machine is broken. (#25)
- The screen of Assembler 2 and 3 shows the fluid box as a gauge beside the energy bar, with the fluid and its amount out of 1,000 mB on hover, and Jade, if installed, shows it as its own fluid bar, out of 1,000 mB. Assembler 1's screen and tooltip are unchanged. (#25)

## 0.4.0

- An Assembling recipe's `results` replaces `result`, which no longer reads, so this breaks a pack's own recipes: it is a list of item stacks, and `"result": {"id": "minecraft:stick"}` becomes `"results": [{"id": "minecraft:stick"}]`. It is required, and written `[]` for a recipe that makes only fluids; an empty `results` with no `fluid_results` fails to load. In KubeJS `assembling(result, ingredients)` takes one stack or a list, so scripts read as before. EMI and JEI show every item and fluid result. (#24)
- An Assembling recipe has a `category`, one of `crafting` (the default), `advanced-crafting`, `crafting-with-fluid`, `chemistry` and `oil-processing`; any other name fails to load, naming it. KubeJS sets it with `.category('chemistry')`, and EMI and JEI show it. Each Assembler tier holds the categories in its `categories` in `craftworks-server.toml`, which take only those five names: tier 1 holds `crafting` and `advanced-crafting`, and tiers 2 and 3 add `crafting-with-fluid`. Fill Recipe on a recipe outside them is refused with a message, and an Assembler Fast Replaced to a tier that doesn't hold its Held recipe's category keeps the recipe and idles as Can't run. (#24)
- An Assembler makes a recipe's first result in the product slot and puts every other result in the remainder slot with the ingredients' remainders, as cake's buckets do. Fill Recipe is refused with the remainder message when those are two different items or more than a stack, and a craft waits in Output full while the remainder slot can't take them. Only a recipe with exactly one item result is Hand-craftable, so the Personal Assembler never plans a recipe with none or several. (#24)
- The Assembler's ghost ingredients, and the Held recipe's product at the head of its screen, answer EMI's and JEI's shortcuts as a real stack does: hover one and press `R` (or left-click in JEI) for the recipe that makes it, or `U` (or right-click in JEI) for what it is used in. A tag ingredient answers with the member it shows at that moment. Clicking a ghost still does what clicking the empty slot does. (#30)
- Jade, if installed, shows an Assembler's tooltip from any block of its footprint: the Held recipe's product, or "No Held recipe"; its state, one of Crafting (with a progress bar, the screen's), Needs power, Missing ingredients, Output full or Can't run; and its energy. The Creative Energy Source shows Infinite FE. Jade is optional, and without it nothing changes. (#28)
- Craftworks has a creative tab of its own, Craftworks (`craftworks:craftworks`), with the Assembler 1 as its icon, holding the Assembler in tiers 1 to 3 and the Creative Energy Source in that order. They no longer sit in the Functional Blocks tab, and creative search still finds them. (#29)
- The Creative Energy Source (`craftworks:creative_energy_source`), a creative-only block for powering an Assembler where no energy mod is installed, such as the dev client: every tick it offers unlimited FE to the energy capability of each block touching it, on all six faces, and its own capability gives FE without limit and accepts none. It has no recipe, so EMI and JEI show none, and it drops itself when broken. Find it in the Craftworks creative tab, with stand-in art for now. (#23)
- The log no longer warns "can't be placed due to empty ingredients" for every Assembling recipe at each load, about a thousand lines in a vanilla world. A recipe with no item ingredient still draws it.
- The Assembler's screen draws the slots of your inventory and hotbar, which were missing, so only their items showed.
- Accepts Groundworks from 0.5.4 up to, but not including, 0.6, since a new minor may break Craftworks.

## 0.3.0

- The Assembler, in tiers 1 to 3 (`craftworks:assembler_1`, `_2`, `_3`): a placed 3x2x3 machine, a 3x3 square two blocks tall, that makes its Held recipe from the items and power it is given, with stand-in art for now, framed in an iron, gold or diamond tint by tier (#22). Open it and press Fill Recipe in EMI or JEI to set the Held recipe; it is never matched from the items put in. Fill Recipe is refused, with a message, for a recipe that is Locked for you (asked once, of whoever presses: the Assembler then keeps making it), one that names a fluid (tiers 2 and 3 take them later), one with more than 5 different ingredients, and one whose leftovers (buckets, bottles) are two different items or more than a stack. Every other Assembling recipe is fine, Hand-craftable or not. Changing the Held recipe hands the inputs back to you. Slot n takes the Held recipe's nth ingredient and the other input slots take nothing; pipes and hoppers can fill an input only up to the Overload Limit, on any face of any block of the Assembler, and only the two outputs, the product and the ingredients' remainders (cake's buckets), extract. A craft waits while either output is full. It takes any FE source: a craft takes the recipe's `time` over the tier's speed in ticks and costs the tier's power times that, paid a little every tick (a tick it can't pay in full makes no progress), and an idle Assembler draws nothing. `craftworks-server.toml` sets each tier's `speed`, `power` and `buffer`: 0.5, 0.75 and 1.25; 37.5, 75 and 187.5 FE a tick (75, 150 and 375 kW); 50,000 FE. Breaking it drops its contents and the item keeps the Held recipe; placing another tier's item over it swaps it whole, keeping both and handing back the old item. Each tier has a default Assembling recipe from vanilla items at its own id, which a pack overrides. Requires Groundworks 0.5.4 or later, which is now a required mod. (#21)

## 0.2.1

- Assembling recipes can name fluids: `fluid_ingredients` (a list of `{ingredient: "minecraft:water", amount}`) and `fluid_results` (a list of `{id, amount}`), both optional, and `hand_craftable`, default true. The Personal Assembler plans only through a Hand-craftable recipe: `hand_craftable` true and no fluid in or out. Any other never appears in a Crafting Plan, and EMI and JEI still list it. A recipe written before these fields reads unchanged. KubeJS sets them with `.fluidIngredients([Fluid.of('minecraft:water', 250)])`, `.fluidResults([...])` and `.handCraftable(false)`. (5thlayer/factoryworks#578)

## 0.2.0

- A request whose plan the Assembler would have to search endlessly for, such as a banner when you hold nothing towards any of its dyes, stops after 5,000 tries and shows as Missing, where it used to freeze the server. (#17)
- EMI: the Craftables list shows an Assembling recipe exactly when it is Ready: one craft resolves with nothing Missing or Locked, intermediates included, so sticks are listed when you hold only logs, and a Locked recipe is not. Both routes to an item are listed when both are Ready. Fill Recipe stays lit whatever you hold. The server works it out only while your inventory screen is open, a few ticks after your inventory settles and again every two seconds (which catches a lock lifting), within about 2 ms of a tick, so a large pack takes several ticks to update. Written for EMI 1.1.24 on 26.1.2: a different EMI logs an error and lists as before. (#17)
- EMI: `@craftworks` in the search finds every item an Assembling recipe makes, Locked or not. A row of tabs above EMI's item index holds saved searches: clicking one writes its query into the search bar, and it lights while its query is in the search. Craftworks ships an Assembling tab (`@craftworks`). A resource pack adds, replaces or removes tabs with `assets/<namespace>/craftworks/tabs/<id>.json` (`icon`, `name`, `query`, `order`, or `"enabled": false`), and mods register them with `IndexTabs.register`. Written for EMI 1.1.24 on 26.1.2: a different EMI logs an error and behaves as stock. (#15)
- Other mods' shaped and shapeless crafting recipes are Assembling recipes too: they convert at their own ids as recipes load, after datapacks and KubeJS, with the default time and priority, and a pack's own Assembling recipe at the same id wins. `modRecipesExcluded` in `craftworks-server.toml` keeps namespaces or recipe ids at the crafting table, and `modRecipes = false` turns it off; a pack that relied on mods' crafting staying at the table must set one. (#13)
- JEI support, optional: Assembling recipes have their own JEI category, and a recipe button beside each queues it on the Personal Assembler as EMI's Fill Recipe does. Left-click queues 1, right-click 5, Shift-click as many as you can afford, middle-click shows the Crafting Plan. Needs JEI 29.20.0.53 or later. (#12)
- KubeJS support, optional: scripts create and edit Assembling recipes with `event.recipes.craftworks.assembling(result, ingredients)`, plus `.time(ticks)` and `.priority(n)`, and lock a recipe for a player from a server script with `CraftworksEvents.lock(event => { ... event.lock(reason) })`, combined with the other Lock sources. Needs KubeJS 26.1.2-8.0.3 or later. (#11)
- Craftworks is playable out of the box: vanilla's crafting recipes are Assembling recipes at their own ids, so the crafting table no longer makes them instantly. Special recipes (fireworks, dyeing, map cloning, repair and the like) stay at the crafting table. A pack turns this off with `vanillaRecipes = false` in `craftworks-server.toml`; a pack that relied on vanilla crafting staying at the table must set it. Its own recipes at vanilla's ids still win over the built-in ones. (#9)
- Several Assembling recipes can make the same item. The Assembler takes the highest `priority` route that resolves with nothing Missing or Locked, ties going to the lowest recipe id, and falls back down the list: sticks come from bamboo when there are no planks. (#8)

## 0.1.3

- The jar carries its MIT licence.
- The inventory no longer shows a white sliver of the removed crafting grid's result slot beside the queue. (#19)

## 0.1.2

- A Locked recipe in the Crafting Plan can say why, for example "Research: Steel Axe", beside the entry and in the "Can't start" line. With `lockSources = ["researchd"]` it names the research that unlocks the recipe. Mods and packs can give reasons through `LockHooks.registerReasoned`. (#18)

## 0.1.1

- The Crafting Plan: a middle click on Fill Recipe opens it, and so does a request your inventory can't cover, naming why (Missing, Locked or not enough). It lists what a craft consumes, the intermediates it makes, and what is Missing and Locked. +1, +5 and All queue straight away and grey out when your inventory can't cover them; the plan stays open and updates as you queue, showing the first queue rows. Close returns to the inventory. (#7)
- Fill Recipe on a Locked recipe now opens the Crafting Plan showing it Locked, rather than queueing nothing silently. (#10)

## 0.1.0

- Assembling recipes: a datapack can add `craftworks:assembling` recipes (ingredients with counts and tags, one result, `time` in ticks defaulting to 10, `priority` defaulting to 0). EMI shows them in an Assembling category. (#4)
- The Personal Assembler queues and crafts: on the inventory screen, EMI's Fill Recipe on an Assembling recipe queues 1 (left click), 5 (right click) or as many as your inventory covers (Shift). The whole cost is taken up front, each step takes its craft time, and results and ingredient remainders arrive in your inventory. A full inventory pauses the queue until there is room. The queue survives logging out, is shown beside the hotbar while the inventory is closed, and is refunded into your inventory when you die, before your items drop. (#5)
- The inventory screen is the Assembler: the 2x2 crafting grid and the recipe book button are gone, and the queue is drawn where the grid was, two rows and a "+N more" line. Click either icon of a row to cancel crafts of its final recipe: left 1, right 5, Shift all; what is left of a row is planned again in place. The grid's slots stay in the inventory menu, so slot indices other mods use are unchanged. A client now needs EMI or JEI installed, and refuses to start without one; a dedicated server needs neither. (#6)
- Lock source: the server config `lockSources` lists what locks a recipe for a player: `recipeBook` (Locked until the recipe is in their recipe book) and `researchd` (Locked while Researchd blocks it for their team). Empty, the default, locks nothing. Mods add their own locks through `io.github._5thlayer.craftworks.api.LockHooks`. A recipe is Locked if any listed source or any hook says so, and Fill Recipe on a Locked recipe queues nothing. (#10)
